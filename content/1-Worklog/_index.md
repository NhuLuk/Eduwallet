## Introduction

During the **8-week internship** in the **AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I had the opportunity to learn and practice both fundamental and advanced concepts of Amazon Web Services (AWS). Through a combination of theoretical lessons and hands-on labs, I gained practical experience in designing, deploying, and managing cloud-based solutions.

Alongside the training sessions, I analyzed, designed, and developed the proposal for **EduWallet**, a personal finance management application for students built on AWS. Throughout the internship, I strengthened my knowledge of Cloud Computing, Networking, Security, Databases, Monitoring, and Solution Architecture while improving my research, self-learning, and problem-solving skills.
