## Reflection on the Internship

During the **8-week AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship, I had the opportunity to gain valuable knowledge and practical experience with Amazon Web Services (AWS). Beyond learning technical concepts, the program also helped me develop self-learning skills, research abilities, and problem-solving approaches in a real-world learning environment. These experiences have been meaningful milestones in my academic journey and future career development.

---

## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Learning Environment

The program provided a professional, well-organized, and encouraging learning environment. The learning roadmap was clearly structured, allowing participants to gradually build knowledge from fundamental AWS concepts to more advanced cloud technologies.

The combination of self-study, hands-on practice, and mentor guidance helped me understand the topics more deeply and apply what I learned to a practical project.

---

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Organizing Team

The mentors were always willing to answer questions and provide guidance whenever I encountered difficulties during the internship.

Instead of giving direct solutions, they often encouraged me to analyze problems independently, search for documentation, and develop my own solutions. This approach significantly improved my critical thinking and self-learning abilities.

The organizing team also kept participants informed with timely announcements, learning materials, and schedules throughout the program.

---

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Background

The internship content was highly relevant to my major in Information Technology, especially in the field of Cloud Computing.

Through this program, I was introduced to AWS services such as Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, Amazon RDS, Amazon Cognito, and Amazon CloudWatch, while also learning the fundamentals of Solution Architecture. These experiences helped me better understand how cloud systems are designed and deployed in real-world scenarios.

---

### 4. Learning and Professional Development

The internship provided more than technical knowledge.

Throughout the program, I learned how to analyze project requirements, design cloud architectures, prepare technical proposals, and develop workshop documentation.

In addition, I strengthened essential soft skills such as self-learning, technical research, time management, teamwork, and technical documentation.

These skills will be valuable as I continue my studies and prepare for my future career.

---

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

One aspect I appreciated most was the supportive learning culture among mentors and participants.

Everyone was willing to share knowledge, exchange ideas, and help each other solve technical challenges. This collaborative atmosphere created a positive learning experience and encouraged me to participate actively throughout the internship.

---

### 6. Overall Impression of the Program

AWS First Cloud AI Journey is a well-designed internship program with a structured learning path and practical content.

It not only introduced me to AWS services but also provided the opportunity to apply cloud knowledge through a real-world project. The experience has strengthened my interest in Cloud Computing and provided a solid foundation for further learning and professional development.

---

# Additional Questions

## What did you enjoy the most during the internship?

The most valuable part of the internship was the opportunity to learn AWS through a structured learning path while developing a practical cloud project.

Working on the EduWallet project allowed me to understand the complete workflow of requirement analysis, solution architecture design, and selecting appropriate AWS services for different business requirements.

---

## What could be improved for future interns?

I would appreciate having more hands-on workshops and technical sharing sessions from cloud professionals who have practical industry experience.

Additionally, introducing small projects or technical challenges after each learning module would help participants reinforce their understanding and apply newly acquired knowledge more effectively.

---

## Would you recommend this program to your friends?

Yes.

I would definitely recommend AWS First Cloud AI Journey to students who are interested in Cloud Computing and Amazon Web Services. The program offers a clear learning roadmap, high-quality learning materials, practical projects, and valuable guidance from experienced mentors.

---

# Suggestions and Future Expectations

- Organize more hands-on AWS workshops throughout the program.
- Provide additional mini-projects or technical exercises after each learning topic.
- Invite Solution Architects and Cloud Engineers to share their real-world project experiences.
- Create more networking and discussion sessions between mentors and interns.

If the program offers advanced courses in AWS, DevOps, or Solution Architecture in the future, I would be delighted to participate again to further improve my technical knowledge and professional skills.

---

## Acknowledgements

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the AWS First Cloud AI Journey organizing team and all mentors for their dedication, guidance, and continuous support throughout the internship.

Although the internship lasted only eight weeks, it provided me with valuable knowledge, practical experience, and motivation to continue pursuing a career in Cloud Computing. I believe the lessons and experiences gained from this program will serve as a strong foundation for my future academic and professional journey.
